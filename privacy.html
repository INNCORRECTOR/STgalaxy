Privacy Policy

We are a locally-owned phone accessories retailer operating from Nagaland, India. We are not affiliated with any large technology company. This page explains, in plain language, what happens when you use our website or message us.

Last updated: June 2026

What we collect

This website does not ask you to create an account or fill out order forms. If you contact us on WhatsApp, Instagram, or email, you choose what to share — usually your name, phone number, and delivery details so we can help with your order.

If you buy any products from us, you get access to our Customers-Only group where we use that for updates on offers and giveaways only for our customers.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't run ad trackers or analytics on this site.
  • We don't send marketing spam without your consent.

Website & third-party links

Our pages may load fonts from Google Fonts to look nice in your browser. When you tap WhatsApp or Instagram, you leave our site and their privacy rules apply. Product photos may come from our own uploads or stock images shown for reference.

How long we keep information

We keep order-related messages only as long as we need them for business records, support, or warranty — then we delete them when it is reasonable to do so.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, or request that we update or delete it. Just message or email us — we're happy to help.
